Exhibit C: Flow simulation (2D, live wing shape)

m/s
Wing changed, updating simulation…
Air flows left to right past the current wing shape and angle of attack (left and right servos averaged). Speed and vorticity show the flow field as colour. Wind view shows moving tracer particles carried by the simulated air (blue = slow, white = free stream, yellow = accelerated), so you can see where the wind is deflected. When the wing morphs, the simulation waits about 1 s for it to settle, then restarts; changing tunnel speed just adjusts the flow smoothly. In speed and vorticity view the pale moving bands are smoke pulses carried by the air. Qualitative only: 2D, low Reynolds number (the sim cannot reach your tunnel's real Re, so tunnel speed changes the flow speed shown but not the Re), and Cl/Cd are rough indicators, not results to quote.
